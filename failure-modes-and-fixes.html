Failure Modes & Fixes

The bugs that reach production, as a lookup table. Each card gives the symptom you’ll actually see, a diagram you can flip between Broken and Fixed, the .NET / React / Azure fix, and how to catch it before users do.

The loop behind every fix

Skip a step and the bug comes back: prevent means a test, an alert, or a constraint that makes the failure impossible to reintroduce.

How to read a card
