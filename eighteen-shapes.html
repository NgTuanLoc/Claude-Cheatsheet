Eighteen shapes behind a hundred bugs

The long sheet has one hundred and six problems and two hundred diagrams. Underneath them are eighteen mechanisms. Learn the shape and one diagram covers a dozen bugs — including the ones nobody has written a card for yet.

How to use it

  • DebuggingSearch the symptom. The matching shape tells you what kind of mistake it is before you know which line caused it.
  • ReviewingRun the eighteen questions over a design or a pull request. Each one is the question that shape exists to make you ask.
  • Each shapeThe law it breaks, one diagram, how it shows up, the move that fixes it — and the problems it accounts for.
